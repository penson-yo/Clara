Clara

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what data Clara collects, how it is used, and what choices users have.

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Clara is a voice-first note capture app designed to help users quickly offload thoughts, tasks, ideas, and reflections into a personal vault.

1. Information Clara Collects

Clara may collect the following information when you use the app:

  • voice transcripts created from audio you record in the app
  • Clara’s generated replies
  • note routing and filing information, such as which area of the vault a note was saved to
  • session history used to understand long conversations over time
  • app version, build number, and a random install identifier

During beta testing, Clara may also collect session log data to improve prompt quality, routing accuracy, and long-conversation performance.

2. Beta Session Log Sharing

During beta testing, Clara may upload session logs to help improve the product.

These logs may include:

  • transcripts of what you said to Clara
  • Clara’s replies
  • note titles and destinations
  • session history and timing information
  • app version and build information

This data is not tied to your account. It is associated only with a random install identifier generated by the app.

Users can turn beta session log sharing off at any time in the app’s Settings.

3. How Clara Uses Information

Clara uses collected information to:

  • transcribe and process voice input
  • route entries into the correct part of the vault
  • improve prompt design, routing quality, and response quality
  • diagnose bugs, reliability issues, and product problems
  • improve long-conversation behavior during beta testing

Clara does not use beta session logs to create advertising profiles or sell user data.

4. Storage and Processing

Notes created in Clara are primarily stored in the user’s own vault, including iCloud-backed storage when enabled.

Some app requests may be processed by third-party AI and infrastructure providers in order to deliver transcription, routing, and beta telemetry functionality.

5. User Choices

Users can:

  • choose whether to share beta session logs
  • turn beta session log sharing off in Settings at any time
  • delete local notes and session history from within the app

Turning off beta session log sharing stops future uploads. It does not automatically remove data that was already shared during beta testing.

6. Data Retention

Beta session data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to improve Clara, investigate bugs, review product behavior, and support the beta program.

Local notes and local session history remain under the user’s control unless deleted by the user.

7. Third-Party Services

Clara may rely on third-party service providers to operate parts of the app, such as:

  • AI model providers for transcription or routing
  • infrastructure providers for backend storage and beta telemetry
  • Apple services such as iCloud and TestFlight

If you use a TestFlight build, Apple may also collect diagnostic and beta testing information under its own terms and policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

Clara is not intended for children under 13, and the app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If material changes are made, the updated version will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Clara’s data practices, contact hello@heyclara.co.
